Character Sheets

Fill in, save and print Pathfinder 2e characters on three pages, with every modifier worked out from your attributes, ranks and level.

🧭 Import from Pathbuilder 2e
  1. In Pathbuilder 2e, open your character and choose Menu → Export → Export JSON. It shows a six-digit number.
  2. Enter the number here and open your export. It opens in a new tab on Pathbuilder's site.
  3. Select everything on that page (Ctrl+A or ⌘A), copy it, paste it below and press Import. If you saved the export as a file instead, use Choose File.

Pathbuilder's site checks for real browsers, so this page can't fetch the export by itself; copying it across takes a few seconds. Importing the same character again updates its sheet. Pathbuilder 2e is a separate app by Redrazors; this tool is unofficial.

How it works. A sheet auto-saves in this browser once it has a name. Renaming a sheet renames its save. Use the tabs to move between the three pages. Print prints all three pages of the current character, one per US Letter sheet, and Print Blank prints an empty set. Export downloads every sheet as a JSON file and Import merges sheets back in; it also accepts a saved Pathbuilder export. Nothing leaves your browser.

Worked out for you. Enter attribute modifiers (the Remaster way). Each skill, save and Perception adds its attribute modifier, its proficiency bonus and any item bonus. The proficiency bonus is your level plus 2 (trained), 4 (expert), 6 (master) or 8 (legendary), and 0 if untrained. Class DC and spell DC are 10 + attribute + proficiency; spell attack is attribute + proficiency. AC depends on your armor, so enter it yourself. See Character Creation for building a character and the Dice Roller to roll with these numbers.
