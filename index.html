The Pathfinder 2e Guide

Pathfinder Second Edition from Paizo is a fantasy roleplaying game with deep character building and clean, tactical turns: three actions, one reaction, and four degrees of success on every roll. This 17-part guide follows the Remaster rules (Player Core and GM Core) and takes you from your first character to running whole campaigns.

Pathfinder 2e at a Glance

Four ideas carry most of the game. Each part of the guide builds on them.

Three Actions

On your turn you get three actions and one reaction. Move, Strike, cast a spell (most take two actions) or Raise a Shield: mix them however you like.

Degrees of Success

Roll d20 + modifier against a DC. Beat it by 10 for a critical success; miss by 10 for a critical failure. A natural 20 or 1 shifts the result one step.

Proficiency

Trained, expert, master and legendary add 2, 4, 6 or 8 plus your level, so everyone grows every level and the best get much better.

Build Choices

Ancestry, heritage, background and class, then a feat at almost every level. That's where the game's famous customization lives.
